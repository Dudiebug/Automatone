# M4.3 — Make Stop simple, synchronous, and idempotent

## Metadata

- State: PLANNED
- Milestone: M4
- Depends on: INFERRED M4.2 scheduling edge; no explicit task-level dependency
  is stated by the plan.
- Risk: critical
- Sensor profiles: default, architecture_sensitive, runtime_minecraft
- Approved plan reference: plan lines 516-524.

## Objective

Expose one server-owned Stop path that cancels native MineProcess, clears worker
break/input state, is harmless when repeated, and cannot be undone by stale
async work.

## Why this task exists

Cancellation is a safety and correctness boundary. The product needs a small
idempotent method around native cancellation, not a second process or a task
queue, so Stop cannot leave stale movement or finish a stale block.

## Allowed scope

- One product Stop method.
- Native cancel plus worker break/input clearing.
- Harmless idle/repeated Stop.
- Busy Start rejection.
- Stale async invalidation.

## Explicitly forbidden / out of scope

- Task replacement, job queues, automatic retries, or offline mining.
- A second consumer cancellation/mining engine.
- Client animation, packet identity, or GUI as Stop authority.
- Storage, dashboards, fleets, or process-internal persistence.

The mandatory shared rules are in INDEX.md#global-architecture-contract,
INDEX.md#explicitly-out-of-scope-through-milestone-6, and
INDEX.md#milestone-4-gate.

## Implementation constraints

- Native MineProcess.cancel() remains the native authority.
- Product Stop is synchronous from the server's state perspective and
  idempotent.
- Clear worker break/input state and invalidate stale work.
- Reject busy Start; do not add queues or replacement.
