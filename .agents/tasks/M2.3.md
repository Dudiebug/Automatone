## Objective

Make native Automatone movement input control the worker deterministically on
the server without packets, fake clients, jitter, or vanilla controls
overwriting it.

## Why this task exists

The worker is not a player and must not require fake-client input
infrastructure. It must apply the movement decisions already produced by
native Automatone while resolving control conflicts locally on the worker.

This task proves direct movement, not custom pathfinding. Native goals and
pathing remain Automatone-owned.

## Allowed scope

- Verify Automatone forward, sideways, jump, and look input moves the custom
  entity.
- Prevent vanilla goals, navigation, MoveControl, LookControl, and JumpControl
  from overwriting active Automatone decisions.
- Prefer solving conflicts in WorkerEntity, not global mixins.
- Define inactive behavior as simply standing still.
- Confirm cancel clears stale movement.

## Explicitly forbidden / out of scope

- Fake clients, fake players, packet-driven movement, or client authority.
- Global mob-control mixins when worker-local control can express the invariant.
- Custom pathfinding or movement scripts.
- Combat, wandering, following, storage, GUI, networking, quantity, or
  persistence behavior.
- GUI work before Milestone 3 passes.

The full global architecture contract, quantity semantics, and excluded-feature
list are preserved in INDEX.md.

## Implementation constraints

- Native Automatone remains the source of movement decisions.
- Apply movement on the server/main thread through the worker.
- Resolve control conflicts locally where possible.
- Inactive means standing still.
- Cancellation must clear stale movement input and not modify world state.
