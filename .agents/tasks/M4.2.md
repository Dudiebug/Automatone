# M4.2 — Implement finite and unlimited execution

## Metadata

- State: PLANNED
- Milestone: M4
- Depends on: INFERRED M4.1 scheduling edge; no explicit task-level dependency
  is stated by the plan.
- Risk: high
- Sensor profiles: default, architecture_sensitive, runtime_minecraft
- Approved plan reference: plan lines 106-119 and 507-514.

## Objective

Wrap native mining with server-authoritative finite source-block stopping or
unlimited continuation, stopping exactly on the Nth matching destruction.

## Why this task exists

Inventory quantity and source-block quantity diverge with Fortune, Silk Touch,
multi-drops, and pre-existing items. The product must enforce its own source
success count while leaving discovery, pathing, movement, and mining native.

## Allowed scope

- Finite native mining with immediate cancel on the Nth matching source block.
- Unlimited native mining without the finite check.
- Source-block progress independent of inventory and drops.
- Sensible finite amount validation in consumer code.

## Explicitly forbidden / out of scope

- Native inventory/item quantity as product limit.
- Counting drops, Fortune, Silk Touch, pickups, or pre-existing inventory.
- A second scanner, pathfinder, target queue, movement engine, or mining
  algorithm.
- Queues, automatic retries, GUI expansion, storage, or offline mining.

The mandatory shared rules are in INDEX.md#global-architecture-contract,
INDEX.md#quantity-semantics, INDEX.md#explicitly-out-of-scope-through-milestone-6,
and INDEX.md#milestone-4-gate.

## Preflight questions

1. What successful-destruction callback from M4.1 is authoritative?
2. Which native MineProcess start/cancel callers must remain unchanged?
3. Does product code already validate finite amounts?
4. Which task boundary could accidentally turn product quantity into native
   inventory quantity?
5. What GameTest observes five targets, quantity 3, unlimited continuation,
   Fortune/multi-drop, and pre-existing inventory?
6. What finite upper bound is approved? If absent, record UNKNOWN.

## Implementation constraints

- Count matching source blocks only.
- Call native cancel immediately at the Nth success.
- Unlimited has no finite check but remains bounded by Stop/failure/exhaustion.
- Keep amount validation in the consumer and do not invent undocumented
  policy.
- Preserve one native mining path.
